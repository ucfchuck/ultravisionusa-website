How it works

Three steps from a scan to a smarter scan

Browse and install

Choose measurement packages and workflows for your specialty from the marketplace on the system itself. Packages run on the UltraVision's own CPU, GPU and FPGA, or through partner services that analyze your cines on their own servers. Either way they appear in the same marketplace, with the same privacy handling.

Subscribe for updates

Models and calculation packages are refreshed over the system's secure connection, the same channel that already delivers software upgrades and remote support.

Contribute and improve

Choose to contribute anonymized images and measurements. Contributed data retrains the models, and every subscriber gets the better version.

Why on UltraVision

A platform built to be reprogrammed

Every UltraVision routes its data through a Windows PC, a GPU and one very large FPGA. That is why new modes have always arrived as software, and why AI packages can run on the system in real time, next to the image, without extra hardware.

  • Built-in teleradiology and AI compatibility on every system, ready to integrate with SaaS providers
  • Raw RF and acoustic-line data available to model developers through the API
  • Windows security with administrator and sonographer logins
  • Works on the XS and XR today, and on the XC when it ships

Data stays private

Patient identifiers are stripped on the system before anything is uploaded. Designed for HIPAA-compliant workflows, and contribution is always your choice.

Reduces dependence on scarce expertise

Automated measurements support physicians who are conscious of liability and make point-of-care ultrasound practical where sonographers are hard to find.

Open to developers and partners

Have a model, a calculation package or a cloud analysis service? Talk to us about offering it on the marketplace to UltraVision users.
