Shared platform

What every UltraVision has in common

Three processors in ascending order of speed: an Intel i7 for the interface and connectivity, a GPU programmed in DirectX for real-time image formation, and a single very large FPGA for beamforming.

  • Eight simultaneous beamformers for high frame rates at lower transmit rates
  • Spatial compounding, speckle reduction, reverberation reduction and edge enhancement
  • Pinch-to-zoom to less than a square centimetre, up to four images on screen, duplex and triplex
  • One-touch automatic optimization in B, colour and PW modes
  • Remote upgrades, service, support and training over Wi-Fi
  • Windows security: administrator and sonographer logins, encrypted patient data
  • Everything reprogrammable from the PC, including the FPGA, power sequencer and smart transducers
