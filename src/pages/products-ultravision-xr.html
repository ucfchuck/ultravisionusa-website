UltraVision XR

The development system for the researcher or corporate developer with a target product in mind. Designed to be unobtrusive at the lowest cost, with an FDA 510(k) on file, the XR can be the fastest and lowest-cost path to bring a new ultrasound product to market.

Talk to an engineer Specifications
Three connections

Power, Thunderbolt, transducer. That is the whole setup.

Our advice to a developer: buy an Intel i7 laptop with Thunderbolt 3, add our Application Programming Interface, and connect the XR. It ships with our Clinical Ultrasound Program for reference, supporting B-mode, M-mode, pulse-wave Doppler, colour flow Doppler, compression elastography, photoacoustics, harmonic imaging, a patient database, 10-second cine and continuous recording.

  • 12 V DC power from an IEC 60601-1 desktop supply
  • Thunderbolt 3 to the developer's PC, converted internally from PCIe
  • Industry-standard transducer connector; curved linear, linear and phased arrays available

Everything is programmable

The API is written in C# with a C++ wrapper so developers can write their own graphical user interface and control the operation and style of the system.

Transmit
+HV, −HV, ground and high impedance on every channel; 512 delay profiles and 512 waveform profiles at 6 or 4 ns granularity, applied independently to each element
Beamformers
Eight, acquiring acoustic lines simultaneously
Raw data
RF from each beamformer or from each element's channel; 14-bit per channel, 20-bit beamformed, 16-bit enveloped acoustic line amplitude
Digitization
40 or 60 MHz

From algorithm to real time

Develop on the PC, deploy in the FPGA

In the clinical platform the algorithms that produce each mode are shared between the CPU, the GPU and the FPGA. Developers create new algorithms from the RF or acoustic-line data in the CPU and GPU, and when they are perfected the UltraVision engineers instantiate the high-speed components in the FPGA for real-time operation.

The XR differs from, and is not promoted as, a pure research platform. If a custom algorithm can be specified in pulser phase, frequency and delay, it is usually quicker for the UltraVision team to implement it from the system's extensive capabilities than for the developer to start from scratch.
