Education and training

Become proficient and confident with point-of-care ultrasound

Point-of-care ultrasound and injection therapy are operator dependent. UltraVision offers several teaching routes so physicians, assistants and sonographers can develop the skills these exams require: a mobile learning solution, in-office training by MSK experts, and workshops.

Mobile learning

mskNAV

Developed by Dr. Cliff Gronseth, mskNAV is a modern mobile learning solution for ultrasound: all six major joint modules (shoulder, elbow, hand and wrist, hip, knee, foot and ankle), an injections module add-on, and eligibility for AMA PRA Category 1 CME credits. Fully mobile, works offline, delivered on an iPad with built-in anatomy and image labeling for patient education.

In-office training

Dr. Randy Moore, MSK Masters

A pioneer, teacher and author in musculoskeletal sonography with a specialization in regenerative medicine. One- and two-day onsite sessions tailored to each facility, longer programs for residencies and sonography schools, the Sonography of the Extremities textbook and flashcards, and an online core curriculum course.

mskmasters.com
Workshops

Great Lakes Musculoskeletal Ultrasound

MSKUS workshops at their Texas facility and one-on-one training at a physician's own practice. Veronica Rodriguez, RDMS, RMSKS, CEO of Great Lakes MSK US, has 20 years dedicated to MSK ultrasound and helped record the scanning videos on this site.
