Technology

Built for performance

Three processors share the work: an Intel i7 for the interface, a GPU for real-time image formation and one very large FPGA for massively parallel beamforming. Upgrades and new modes are just software.

Single large FPGA

Billions of transistors connect every channel, so modes are added by reprogramming, not re-engineering.

64 channels, 14-bit

Analog to digital conversion at 40 or 60 MHz with raw RF data available on research systems.

Eight beamformers

Simultaneous acoustic lines for faster frame rates and lower transmit rates, reducing patient exposure.

Thunderbolt or PCIe

The scanner appears as memory to a standard Windows PC, tablet or laptop over Thunderbolt 3 and 4.

Image quality

Excellent images in every mode

B-mode, M-mode, pulse-wave and colour flow Doppler, compression elastography, harmonic imaging, photoacoustics and needle visualization, all from the same hardware.
